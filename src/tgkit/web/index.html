              Your browser (WhatsApp, Zalo, ...)

              Let the AI read chats you have open in your own Chrome or Edge, such as WhatsApp Web or Zalo. It opens a chat and reads it; it never types or sends.

              The AI can then read the pages you are signed in to. WhatsApp and Zalo do not officially allow other programs to read chats and could limit an account that does it: use it gently, at your own risk. Opening a chat marks it as read on your phone too.

              When Claude answers

              1. Press Get Claude in Chrome and add it to Chrome or Edge.
              2. Open it once and sign in with the same Claude account.

              When Gemini or GPT answers

              2. Press Get Playwright Extension and add it to Chrome or Edge.
              3. The first time the AI uses it, Chrome asks which tab to share: pick it and press Allow.

              The extension's page shows a token. Paste it here so Chrome stops asking each time.
